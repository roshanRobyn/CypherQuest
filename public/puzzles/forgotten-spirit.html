The stones await their places.
Whispers of the Spirit
I
Where the shrine's four directions find their balance, the Fox keeps watch.
II
The Moon follows the Fox's northern path. Only one silent space separates them.
III
The Moon's western companion bears the colour of the deep winter sky.
IV
On the Moon's road stands the Black stone. Three silent spaces lie between them.
V
The Red stone rests on the 4th row, first square.
VI
Red is not alone. Its Purple companion rests beside it, toward the rising sun.
VII
The Fox looks eastward. After two silent spaces, Green answers its gaze.
VIII
The White stone claims the place where the eastern and southern edges meet.
IX
The Bell rings on White's vertical road. One levels separate its voice from White.
X
Before the Bell can be heard, the Crow watches from above.
XI
The Sun rests on the 4th row, fifth square.
XII
What rises above the Fox casts its opposite below. There the Leaf takes root.
XIII
From the Leaf, follow the shrine floor toward the rising sun. After one silent stone, Rain waits.
XIV
Rain and the Skull belong to the same vertical trail. Between them lies one untouched place.
XV
The Lotus rests on the 9th row, seventh square.
